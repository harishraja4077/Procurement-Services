Our Story

Built On One Belief: Procurement Drives Value

Stackly began in 2008 as a two-person consultancy in Salem, Tamil Nadu with a single client. Today we are a 300+ strong team serving clients across India and 60+ countries, managing $4.8B of client spend every year.

We've never lost sight of our founding promise: put the buyer's interests first, back every decision with data, and deliver savings that show up on the bottom line.

  • 300+ procurement professionals worldwide
  • Head office in Salem, Tamil Nadu
  • Delivery teams across India serving 60+ countries
  • $4.8B+ spend under management
  • Trusted by 1,200+ clients in 60 countries
